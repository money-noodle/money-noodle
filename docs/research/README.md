# Research evidence convention

[`../README.md`](../README.md) owns document roles, authority, and lifecycle. This convention only defines the minimum operational shape of a research assessment.

Research assessments are **dated evidence**. They are not requirements, current-status records, validation reports, decisions, or acceptance authority. Directory placement and commitment do not accept a finding.

Use `YYYY-MM-DD-topic.md` and include:

- status and as-of date;
- the question, scope, and exclusions;
- method, source versions or immutable snapshots, and retrieval dates;
- clearly separated facts, reasoned inferences or recommendations, and unknowns;
- alternatives where they are relevant;
- uncertainty, exclusions, and the largest validity threat;
- a conclusion and concrete triggers to revisit it.

Prefer primary sources and exact immutable snapshots. Identify self-reported, mutable, secondary, or otherwise weaker evidence and explain why it was used. For every quantitative claim, give its source, sample, as-of date, and exclusions, and label it as **reported**, **measured**, **estimated**, **derived**, or **simulated**.

Do not rewrite a dated assessment merely to agree with a later choice. Correct factual errors visibly with a dated correction; when evidence changes materially, add a newer assessment or mark the old one superseded while preserving it. A current authority document must link to research it actually relies on and state what it takes from that evidence.
