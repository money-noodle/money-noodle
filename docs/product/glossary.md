# Money Noodle Product Language Glossary

## Purpose

This is the canonical bridge between Money Noodle's whimsical experience language and precise finance, trading, probability, risk, accounting, authorization, resource, and progression concepts.

A user-facing term does not replace its canonical domain concept. Product surfaces may be playful; code, APIs, schemas, events, infrastructure, logs, metrics, and tests use the canonical term.

## Entry requirements

Add a mapping before introducing a whimsical domain term. Update the mapping in the same change that changes or removes its use. Each entry must state:

- experience term and canonical domain term;
- exact domain definition and concise user-facing explanation;
- applicable capability/risk profile, scope, and funded or simulated context;
- units, calculation, source time, or precision when relevant;
- examples, edge cases, and nearby concepts it must not be confused with;
- the most material risk of misunderstanding;
- lifecycle status: proposed, active, deprecated, or retired;
- replacement term when deprecated.

One experience term must not silently represent multiple domain concepts. Use one consistent Money Noodle term for a domain concept across every project and across web, mobile, desktop, game, and MMO interfaces. A funded context may use the whimsical term only when the mapping preserves exact financial meaning and makes real-money risk readily discoverable. Administrative surfaces default to the canonical term and use the whimsical term only when product context makes it more accurate without ambiguity.

Inline definitions and localization guidance are not yet universal requirements. Each entry must still contain enough canonical explanation for interfaces to make meaning readily discoverable.

The maintainer is the sole final approval authority for new or changed terminology and glossary mappings while the project has one participant. Agents may propose terms but may not mark them active without maintainer approval. Authority may be delegated explicitly as people join; until documented, it remains with the maintainer.
